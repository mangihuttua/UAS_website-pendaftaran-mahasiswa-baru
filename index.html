Why IBBI?

The world is ever-changing, and with that so do the needs of the industry. IBBI is committed to providing only relevant insights of the current workplace dynamic and fostering a curiosity-driven environment to mold the students into lifelong learners. At IBBI, we believe that learning process takes place beyond classrooms, and should be free of the confine of educational closets. With that said, IBBI students are encouraged to engage in activities, competitions, and other social settings to equip them in bracing the vast jungle of real world after they graduate.
